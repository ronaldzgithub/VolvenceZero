# Next evidence campaign recommendation

1. Gate 7 — SSL → Internal RL causal takeover.
2. Gate 8 — wake/sleep background-slow next-session benefit.
3. Gate 9 — M3 and Titans/Hope matched optimizer/gate evidence.
4. Gate 10 — rare-heavy review/import/rollback promotion.
5. Gate 11 — per-user cross-session continuity and swapped-state isolation.

## Gate 7 first packet

- Keep `vz-temporal` as the SSL/Internal-RL owner and `vz-runtime` as the
  evidence harness; do not introduce another policy owner.
- Freeze `full / no-ssl / no-rl / reverse-order / joint-unfrozen` before
  implementation.
- Primary outcomes must cover held-out delayed reward/composition, takeover
  agreement, switch sparsity, family reuse, terminal return, future leakage
  `=0`, token-space RL mutation `=0`, and exact cycle rollback.
- Existing shared trace train/heldout rows may be used for development only.
  Its locked rows have already been consumed and observed during this
  campaign; formal Gate 7 confirmation needs a fresh locked split.
- Resolve the current causal-takeover readiness discrepancy before any locked
  run: the broad algorithm-depth regression currently reports five
  pre-existing pipeline failures around takeover/RL entry. This is a preflight
  blocker, not a reason to weaken Gate 7 assertions.

## Gate 8 second packet

- Generate fresh multi-turn, multi-session episodes with an explicit
  next-session boundary. The two-turn micro-session corpus is not sufficient.
- Freeze `sleep-consolidation / no-sleep / memory-only-sleep /
  policy-only-sleep`.
- Measure next-session cold-start loss, callback/commitment consistency,
  delayed payoff, drift, asynchronous latency, idempotency, owner writeback
  lineage, and checkpoint rollback.
- Keep turn latency and session-post slow-job latency separate. Sleep may
  consume only closed/session-post evidence and may not add prompt context.

## Corpus requirements inherited from this campaign

- variable-length segments and multiple action families;
- context centroids separated enough to make transfer controls identifiable;
- auditable user-prior variation without user fact leakage;
- fresh locked confirmation never used for design or debugging;
- immutable source lineage and frozen substrate fingerprints across arms.
